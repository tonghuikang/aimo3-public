Recall that the condition  

\[
S_n(\alpha)\star\beta=
\begin{cases}
1 & n=k,l\\[2pt]

\end{cases}
\]

\[
A(x)C(x)=x^{k}+x^{l}=x^{p}\bigl(1+x^{d}\bigr),
\qquad 
A(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{8}\alpha(i)x^{i},
\; C(x)=\sum_{j}\beta(j)x^{-j},
\]
so \(A(x)=\pm x^{p}D(x)\) with a divisor \(D(x)\mid 1+x^{d}\).

For a **fixed** positive integer \(d\) the factorisation of a binomial is well‑known:

\[
1+x^{d}=\prod_{\substack{m\mid2d\\m\nmid d}} \Phi_{m}(x),
\]

where \(\Phi_{m}\) is the \(m\)-th cyclotomic polynomial.
Hence every divisor of \(1+x^{d}\) is a product of a *subset* of the cyclotomic factors
\(\Phi_{m}\) with \(m\mid2d,\ m\nmid d\).

\(D\) cannot exceed 8.  Consequently we only need those even indices \(m\) for which

\[
