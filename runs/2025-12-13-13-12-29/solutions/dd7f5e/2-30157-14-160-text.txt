\framebox{A function \(\alpha\in\mathcal F\) is “shifty’’ iff there exist \(\beta\in\mathcal F\) and distinct integers \(k,l\) such that  

\[
S_n(\alpha)\star\beta=
\begin{cases}
1&n\in\{k,l\}\\[2pt]
0&\text{otherwise}
\end{cases}
\qquad\forall n\in\mathbb Z .
\]}

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 1.  Reformulation in terms of polynomials  

Write \(\alpha\) as a Laurent polynomial  

\[
A(x)=\sum_{i=0}^{8}a_i x^i\qquad (a_i=\alpha(i)).
\]

For any \(\beta\) define  

\[
B(x)=\sum_{j\in\mathbb Z}b_j x^j\qquad (b_j=\beta(j)).
\]

Then  

\[
S_n(\alpha)\star\beta=\sum_{i}a_i\,b_{i-n}
\]

is exactly the coefficient of \(x^n\) in the product \(A(x)B(x)\).  
Hence the condition above is equivalent to

\[
A(x)B(x)=x^{k}+x^{l}=x^{k}\bigl(1+x^{m}\bigr),\qquad m=l-k\neq0 .
\tag{1}
\]

Thus a shifty \(\alpha\) exists **iff** there is an integer \(m\neq0\) such that  

\[
A(x)\mid 1+x^{m}\quad\text{in }\mathbb Z[x,x^{-1}].
\tag{2}
\]

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 2.  Divisors of \(1+x^{m}\)

Factorisation over \(\mathbb Z\) gives  

\[
1+x^{m}= \prod_{\substack{d\mid 2m\\ d\nmid m}}\Phi_{d}(x),
\]

where \(\Phi_{d}\) is the \(d\)-th cyclotomic polynomial.  
All irreducible factors are cyclotomic polynomials with **even** index.  
Moreover, for a fixed \(m\) all those indices have the same 2‑adic valuation:
if \(\nu_2(m)=t\) then each divisor \(d\) of the product satisfies \(\nu_2(d)=t+1\).

Consequently a polynomial \(A\) satisfies (2) **iff**  

* \(A\) is a product of distinct cyclotomic polynomials \(\Phi_{n}\) with even \(n\);
* all chosen \(n\) have the same \(\nu_2(n)\) (the same power of two);
* the total degree \(\deg A=\sum\phi(n)\) does not exceed \(8\) (because \(\alpha\) is zero outside \([0,8]\)).

--------------------------------------------------------------------
### 3.  The sum‑of‑coefficients condition
