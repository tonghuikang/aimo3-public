Possibly the count factorizes as product over internal nodes of binomial coefficients: At each internal node, after we have already assigned numbers to its left and right subtrees satisfying internal constraints, to satisfy the node's constraint we need to interleave them such that after sorting each side individually, left side numbers are all larger than right side numbers (componentwise). That essentially means that when we merge the two sorted lists (each of size s), the merged order must have the right side elements always earlier (faster) than the left side elements in the ordering
