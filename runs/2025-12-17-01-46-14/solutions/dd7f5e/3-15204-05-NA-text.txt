All these have coefficients in {-1,0,1}. So any product of distinct such cyclotomic polynomials will have integer coefficients potentially larger in magnitude, as multiplication could produce coefficients beyond ±1. Example: product of Φ_2(x) = x+1 and Φ_4(x) = x^2+1 yields polynomial (x+1)(x^2+1)=x^3 + x^2 + x + 1, coefficients {1,1,1,1}. That's okay.
