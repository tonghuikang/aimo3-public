Now check that the condition also requires that the pattern is exactly 1 at n=k,l and 0 elsewhere for all n ∈ Z. Since product α * β yields exactly 1 + x^d (when we set shift). Since support of α is within 0..8, and β is finite support, the convolution sum is finite. So the pattern holds.

Thus answer 160.

Potential nuance: Could there be α that uses D = 0? No, α cannot be zero because then product always zero not 1's. So not counted.
