For every integer \(n\ge 6\) let  

\[
f(n)=\min\{k\;|\;\exists\;1<d<k,\;d\mid k,\;1+d+k=n\}.
\]

Writing \(N=n-1\) we have \(N=d\,(t+1)\) with \(d>1\) and \(t\ge1\), and then
\(k=N-d=N\bigl(1-\frac1{t+1}\bigr)\).
To minimise \(k\) we must maximise \(d\); therefore the optimal choice is  

\[
d=\frac{N}{p},
\qquad
p=\text{the smallest divisor of }N\text{ larger than }2.
\]

Hence  

\[
f(n)=\frac{p-1}{p}\,(n-1)\qquad (p>2).
\]
