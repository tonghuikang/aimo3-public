But the tree structure suggests independence: at each node, you choose winners set among participants currently in that node; the choices at different nodes are independent because they involve disjoint sets of participants (since nodes are disjoint after previous choices). Indeed after each partition, winners and losers groups are independent for subsequent rounds. So total number is product over all internal nodes.

Actually the binary strings are determined by which group you go at each node: At each node of size 2^{s}, the winners go to the next node of higher score; losers go to lower group. The final binary string for each participant can be seen as the path from root to leaf: at each round, bit indicates whether participant went to the winner group (1) or loser group (0).
