**Key insight:**  
Write the condition as a convolution: for \(\alpha,\beta\in\mathcal F\),

\[
S_n(\alpha)\star\beta=\sum_{t}\alpha(t+n)\beta(t)=\bigl(\alpha *\tilde\beta\bigr)(n),
\]

where \(\tilde\beta(t)=\beta(-t)\).  
Thus we need \(\alpha *\tilde\beta = x^{k}+x^{l}=x^{k}(1+x^{d})\) with \(d=l-k>0\).

Factor out the smallest exponent of \(\alpha\): \(\alpha(x)=x^{m}C(x)\) with \(C(0)\neq0\).  
Since \(\gcd(C,x)=1\), \(C\) must divide \(1+x^{d}\) in \(\mathbb Z[x]\).  
All divisors of \(1+x^{d}\) are products of distinct **even‑index cyclotomic polynomials** \(\Phi_{n}\) (because \(\Phi_{n}\mid x^{d}+1\) iff \(n\) is even).  

Hence every shifty \(\alpha\) is of the form  

\[
\alpha(x)=\pm x^{m}\prod_{i\in S}\Phi_{n_i},
\]

where each \(n_i\) is even, \(\deg\Phi_{n_i}\le8\), the sum of degrees \(\le8-m\), and \(0\le m\le8-\deg\).  

**Even‑index cyclotomics with \(\deg\le8\):**  

\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
n & \deg\Phi_n\\\hline
2&1\\4&2\\6&2\\8&4\\10&4\\12&4\\14&6\\16&8\\18&6\\20&8\\24&8\\30&8
\end{array}
\]
