Compute f / M = ((M + c - 1) * (p - 1) / p) / M = (p-1)/p * (M + c - 1) / M = (p-1)/p * (1 + (c - 1)/M).

Since (c-1)/M is tiny (< 1e-... ), f / M = (p-1)/p + epsilon, where epsilon = (p-1)/p * (c-1)/M, which is < (p-1)/p * (c-1)/M.

Multiplying by t = 2025!, we get t * f / M = t * (p-1)/p + t * epsilon.

Now t * (p-1)/p may not be integer if p does not divide t. Since t = 2025! includes all numbers up to 2025, so any prime p ≤2025 divides t. For p > 2025, p does not necessarily divide t (unless p <=2025). So for c=10162574, p = 5081287 > 2025, thus p does NOT divide t. So t * (p-1)/p is not integer; floor will truncate.

Thus g(c) = floor(t * f / M) / t = floor(t * (p-1)/p + small) / t = floor( t * (p-1)/p + small ) / t.
