1.  Write the condition in polynomial language.  
    For \(\alpha,\beta\in\mathcal F\) define

    \]
    Then  
    \[
    (S_n\alpha)\star\beta=\sum_i\alpha(i+n)\beta(i)=\text{coeff}_{x^{\,n}}(F(x)G(x)).
    \]
    Hence the requirement “\(=1\) for \(n=k,l\) and \(0\) otherwise’’ is exactly  

    \[
    F(x)G(x)=x^{k}+x^{l}=x^{\ell}(1+x^{d}),\qquad d=|k-l|>0 .
    \]

2.  \(F\) is a **polynomial** with non‑negative exponents and \(\deg F\le8\);  
    \(G\) is a Laurent polynomial (finite support) with integer coefficients.  
    Thus \(F\) must be a divisor of a monomial times \((1+x^{d})\).

3.  Factorisation of \(1+x^{d}\) over \(\mathbb Z\):  
    \[
    1+x^{d}= \prod_{m\mid 2d,\;m\nmid d}\Phi_m(x)
          =\prod_{t\mid d_{\text{odd}}}\Phi_{2^{e}t}(x),
    \]
    where \(d=2^{e-1}d_{\text{odd}}\) (\(d_{\text{odd}}\) odd).  
    All irreducible factors have the same 2‑adic exponent \(e\).

    Its constant term is \(1\).

    \[
    F(x)=x^{a}H(x),\qquad a\ge0,\; \deg H\le8-a,
