Given even e = 2m, condition e | 2d and e ∤ d. Since e = 2m, e | 2d is equivalent to m | d. The condition e ∤ d means 2m does not divide d. Since m | d, 2m does not divide d iff the highest power of 2 dividing d is less than the power of 2 dividing 2m. Write d = 2^t * u where u odd; m = 2^s * v where v odd. Then m|d implies s ≤ t and v|u. Then 2m = 2^{s+1} * v. 2m divides d iff s+1 ≤ t, i.e., s < t. So e ∤ d iff s = t. So the condition for inclusion of Φ_{2m} is that m|d and the exponent of 2 in m is exactly t (the exponent of 2 in d). In other words, m must be of the form m = 2^t * v where v | u (odd). So all m dividing d that have the same v2 as d (the maximum possible) appear in the factorization. So indeed, for a given d, all such m have the same v2 = t.
