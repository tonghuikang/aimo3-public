Moreover, we may multiply D (and consequently α) by the unit −1, giving a second function with the same support. Hence each D yields 2·(9−deg(D)) distinct shifty functions.

Summing over all D we obtain

\[
\sum_{D} 2\bigl(9-\deg D\bigr)=2\cdot 80 = 160.
\]

Thus there are exactly \boxed{160} shifty functions.
