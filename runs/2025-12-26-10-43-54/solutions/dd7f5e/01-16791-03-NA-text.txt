For ni to divide d, we need t to be multiple of 2. If t is odd, then v2(d) = ri-1, insufficient for ni's 2-adic valuation (ri). So if we pick t odd, then ni does not divide d. So condition is that d must be a multiple of d_i but not of 2*d_i. Equivalent to t odd.

Thus we need to find a common d such that for each factor, the multiplier t_i (d/d_i) is odd. So d must be common multiple of all d_i with odd ratios.
