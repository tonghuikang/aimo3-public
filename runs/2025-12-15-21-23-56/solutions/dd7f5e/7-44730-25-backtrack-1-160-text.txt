**Idea:**  Write α as polynomial \(A(x)=\sum_{i=0}^8a_i x^i\).  The condition \(S_n(α)\starβ=δ_{n,k}+δ_{n,l}\) is equivalent to finding a Laurent integer polynomial \(D(x)\) with  
\(A(x)D(x)=x^k+x^l\).  Hence \(A\) must divide a binomial \(x^a(1+x^b)\) in \(\mathbb Z[x,x^{-1}]\).  Since monomials are units, \(A=x^c p(x)\) where \(p(0)=±1\) and \(p\mid(1+x^b)\) for some \(b≥1\).

Thus \(p\) is a product of cyclotomic polynomials \(\Phi_n\) with even \(n\) (because \(1+x^b=\prod_{d|2b,d∤b}\Phi_d\)).  All \(\Phi_n\) appearing for a fixed \(b\) share the same 2‑adic valuation \(\nu_2(n)=\nu_2(b)+1\).
