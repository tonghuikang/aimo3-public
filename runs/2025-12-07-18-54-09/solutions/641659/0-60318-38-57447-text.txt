Thus ratio CT*NB/(BT*NE) = (a - 2 t n_x) * NB / ( (2 t n_x) * NE). NB = distance from N to B = sqrt(Nx^2 + Ny^2). NE = distance between N and E (we have E point). So ratio can be expressed in terms of t, n_x, a, NB, NE.

Alternatively, we can compute using coordinates directly.

Thus we can implement this for given n and c (AB length) that solves cyclic condition.
